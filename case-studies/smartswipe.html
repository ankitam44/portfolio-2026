02 / The Ask

Generic cards, generic accounts.

IDFC Bank's credit cards and savings accounts looked like everyone else's, built for an average customer who doesn't exist. The brief: revamp the pre-sales digital experience for both products to match the personalization customers already expect everywhere else in their lives, and use it to win a new generation of Indian banking customers.

"Building the future of digital banking experience that aims to win over the hearts and minds of a new generation of Indians." Project brief, IDFC Bank
03 / My Role

Two products, one month, one researcher-designer.

UX design and research, solo, against a one-month clock. That meant compressing what's usually three separate workstreams (competitive and psychometric research, persona definition, and full UX for two distinct products) into a single sprint, without either product feeling like the one that got less attention.

The constraint forced a discipline that's stayed with me since: build the personalization logic once, then let two different journeys run on it.

04 / Process

Four beats, four weeks.

01

Discovery & opportunity

Audited competitor products, market trends, and psychometric research to find where digital banking was leaving personalization on the table, and where IDFC could win on it.

Competitor auditPsychometric researchMarket analysis
02

Personas & focus areas

Split users by what actually drives their money behavior (travel, security, salary stage, life goals), then set design principles per product: Play to Benefits, Personalize, Engaging for the card; Promote Healthy Saving, Facilitate, Goal-Based for the account.

2 personas6 focus areas
03

Credit card strategy

A customize-and-match logic: income and occupation feed a benefits picker, which feeds a reward-points calculator and a ranked, percentage-matched card recommendation.

CustomizeCalculateMatch
04

Designed in parallel

Built both flows side by side on a shared screen library and component set, so personalization reads as one coherent bank, not two disconnected features bolted together.

05 / The Work

Two journeys, one personalization engine.

The credit card experience and the savings account experience needed to feel like siblings, not strangers: same trust signals, same personalization logic, different goals.

Credit card - customize & match

Credit Card Landing Page

The engaging credit card landing page for IDFC Bank customers involves several key components to effectively communicate their benefits and features.

Personalised offers

Users are shown offers on the basis of their benefits selection. This would urge them to see their interests at a discounted price and pick a card.

Reward points

Based on the benefits that the user is interested in, the user can calculate their reward points and make a more informed decision.

Matched benefits

This unique feature provides a clear view into the number of chosen benefits that are matching to the recommended IDFC cards for the user's needs, plus additional benefits of the card.

Savings account - goal & match

Savings Landing Page

An efficient savings account landing page for IDFC Bank involves elements designed to attract potential customers and give them the information they need to make an informed decision.

Integrating options

Showing the other beneficial options available at IDFC, which can be availed directly through the savings account, motivates the user to commit.

Cross selling

Showcasing other products, such as a loan related to a savings goal for education, helps promote another product and gives the user a goal-centric experience.

06 / Outcome

Design impact

Measured against the scope of a one-month concept sprint, not a live product, but a complete, testable personalization framework for both journeys.
